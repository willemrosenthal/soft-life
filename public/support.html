Bloop Life support

The Game of Life, with cells that merge into soft blobs.

Get in touch

Found a bug, have an idea, or need help? Open an issue on GitHub. It helps to say which iPhone and iOS version you're using, and what you did just before the problem.

Common questions

How do I draw?

Tap or drag anywhere on the board. Starting a stroke on a living cell erases instead. The brush button on the rail sets how wide the stroke is.

Where did the tools go?

Swiping the rail off the right edge hides it, for a clear board. Swipe in from the right edge, or tap the small tab there, to bring it back.

Why did everything die out, or fill the whole screen?

That's the rule at work. Conway's rule tends to settle into small still shapes and blinkers. Other rules, or your own from the Rule lab, can grow forever or die at once. Tap random soup to start again.

How do I make my own rule?

Open Settings, then Rule, then Make your own rule. Switch neighbour counts on and off and watch the preview. The (i) button explains how rules work.

How do I make my own theme?

Open Settings, then Theme, then New. Pick a background and six cell colours; everything else is worked out from them.

Will I lose my themes and rules?

They're saved on your device and kept when you update the app. Deleting the app deletes them.

Does Bloop Life collect any data?

No. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers. See the privacy policy.
