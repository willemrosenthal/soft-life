Bloop Life privacy policy

Bloop Life does not collect, store or send any personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, ads or trackers, and it makes no network requests while you use it.

Your settings, themes and rules are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

When you share a pattern, the pattern is turned into a link, and you choose where that link goes using your device's share sheet. Bloop Life does not see or keep what you share.

If this policy changes, this page will be updated.

Questions? See Bloop Life support.
